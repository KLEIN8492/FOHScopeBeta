FOHScope for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later)

1. Drag FOHScope onto Applications.
2. First launch: macOS blocks apps that are not notarised. Open FOHScope once, then go to
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "FOHScope was blocked" and click
   Open Anyway. (macOS 13-14: right-click FOHScope > Open.)
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FOHScope.app
3. Allow microphone access when asked, or every input reads silence.
4. CMD+, opens Setup: add an input, pick its interface and channel.
